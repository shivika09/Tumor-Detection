MRI Tumor Screener

Classroom demo, not a diagnosis. The model only separates two classes, so it will answer "tumor" or "no tumor" for any image, including ones that are not brain MRIs. Use real axial brain slices (JPG, PNG, WebP).

How to read the score. The score is the SVM's signed distance from its decision boundary. Above 0 means tumor, below 0 means no tumor. It is not a probability. Scores within ±0.3 of zero are marked low confidence. The sample scans were held out from training; one wrong call per class is included on purpose.
